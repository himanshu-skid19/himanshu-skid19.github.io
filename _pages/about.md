Hey! I'm Himanshu, a Research Fellow at **Microsoft Research**, working with the **ReFoRM** team. I graduated from the **Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati** with a degree in Data Science and AI, and I'm passionate about Machine Learning (ML), Deep Learning (DL), Natural Language Processing (NLP), Computer Vision (CV), and Large Language Models (LLMs).

My expertise lies in **Agentic AI systems**, **Retrieval pipelines**, and **diffusion models**. My Bachelor's Thesis focused on learning richer representations for sequential/temporal data by reframing it through image-based representations, leveraging Vision Transformers. I've also worked on various projects, represented my college in two Inter IIT Tech Meets, and completed research internships at **Arizona State University** and **Adobe Research**.

My current research interests lie in **Information Retrieval** and developing **effective representations for learning** — building on my broader focus on efficient, scalable ML systems.
